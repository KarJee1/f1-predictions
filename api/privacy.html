Privacy policy

F1 Predictions · Effective 29 October 2026

F1 Predictions collects as little as possible and never sells or shares your information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You can use everything except friend groups without an account.

What stays on your device

Your finishing-order picks, your light or dark mode setting, and a copy of the latest predictions are saved on your device so the app works offline. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you sign in for friend groups

Friend groups use Sign in with Apple. When you sign in we store:

  • an account identifier from Apple (we don't receive or store your email address),
  • the display name you choose,
  • your finishing-order picks for each race, and the points they score,
  • which groups you belong to.

This is used only to run friend groups and their leaderboards. Members of your groups see your display name, your points, and your picks for a race once picks for it have locked. The data is stored in Google Cloud.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account, picks, scores and group memberships from our servers straight away.

What the app downloads

The app downloads public race predictions, standings and news headlines from our server, which runs on Google Cloud. Like any web server, it receives the technical details of each request, such as your IP address. These are used only to deliver the data and are kept briefly in Google Cloud's standard logs, never linked to you or used for anything else.

Links to other sites

News headlines open the publisher's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Friend groups are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Everything else in the app works without collecting anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the app's App Store page.

F1 Predictions is an unofficial fan app and is not associated with Formula 1, the FIA or any team.
